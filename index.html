Dallas Wymes

Founder, Silk Road · Listed in Marquis Who's Who

Dallas has spent 35 years in supply chain and logistics, trained across every area of the business: warehousing, import and export air and ocean freight, domestic transport, and customs. He's booked the container, fought the detention charge, run the dock, and cleared the shipment.
