Questions partners ask

Straight answers

What counts as a qualified lead?

A decision maker at a business with a real supply chain or operations problem: a 3PL or warehouse that isn't working, a fulfillment setup to build or fix, freight spend to get under control, or a leadership gap that needs a seasoned hand. Growing e-commerce brands, manufacturers, and distributors are the sweet spot. You introduce them; we qualify them on the first call.

When and how do I get paid?

Commissions are calculated on net revenue as Silk Road receives it from the client and paid monthly. Net revenue means everything the client pays, setup and implementation fees included, less payment processing fees. You can see every line, calculated and paid, in your partner dashboard.

Why do projects pay 20% instead of the tier rate?

The tier schedule is built for accounts that pay every month for years. A consulting project or a fractional leadership role has a defined scope and end date, so it pays a flat 20% of everything the client pays over the life of the project. If the same client later signs an ongoing engagement, that account goes on the tier schedule from its own start date.

Do I have to sell anything?

Not as a referral partner. You make the introduction and Dallas runs the sale and the engagement. Contracted sales partners take a bigger share on ongoing accounts because they work the prospect from first contact through handoff, using decks and proposals Silk Road provides.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. There is no fee, no quota, and no minimum. We sign a short partner agreement, you get a partner code and a referral link, and you earn when businesses you introduce become paying clients.

Who gets credit if two people introduce the same business?

The partner who registered the account first. Booking through your referral link registers it to you automatically; a lead you send by email is logged against your code the day it arrives. A registered account counts as yours if it signs and makes its first payment within 180 days of registration.

Does the same code work for Silk Road AI Solutions?

Yes. One code and one referral link cover both brands. If the business you introduce buys AI systems from Silk Road AI Solutions (lead follow-up, customer communication, document processing, dashboards), that account pays on the tier schedule above. Learn more at silkroadaisolutions.com/partners.
